# Apple Music Discord Presence v0.4.6

The final Apple Music-to-Discord lockup now uses the familiar Apple Music app icon instead of the Apple company silhouette.

## Highlights

- Red Apple Music app icon with the white musical note in the final CTA.
- Clear separation between the AM Presence product logo, service logos, and operating-system marks.

## Downloads

| Platform | Download | Requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| macOS | `apple-music-discord-presence-macos-arm64.zip` | Apple Silicon, macOS 13+ |
| Windows | `apple-music-discord-presence-windows-x64.zip` | Windows 10 1809+ or Windows 11, x64 |

Extract the ZIP, keep the folder somewhere permanent, and run `Install.command` on macOS or `Install.cmd` on Windows.

## What changed

### Fixed

- Replaced the Apple silhouette in the CTA with the recognizable red Apple Music icon and white musical note.
- Recorded the Apple Music icon source alongside the existing platform-icon attribution.
- Bumped the stylesheet cache key so the corrected service icon appears immediately after deployment.

## Verification

- macOS build, Swift helper, TypeScript, and automated tests.
- Manual Browser/IAB checks of the final CTA at desktop and mobile widths.
- Verified the AM Presence product logo remains unchanged in the header, footer, settings screen, and favicon.

## Known limitations

- The macOS build is not signed or notarized, so the first launch may require **Right-click → Open**.
- End-to-end playback still requires Apple Music and desktop Discord running on the user's computer.

## Upgrade notes

No migration required.
