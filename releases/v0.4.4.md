# Apple Music Discord Presence v0.4.4

The public website now follows the image-led composition of the approved concepts much more closely, while keeping its text and download controls responsive and accessible.

## Highlights

- An illustrated Apple Music → local bridge → Discord feature flow.
- Full-scene macOS and Windows download cards with authentic platform marks.
- A connected three-step setup timeline on desktop and mobile.
- A glass download panel with the official Discord symbol.

## Downloads

| Platform | Download | Requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| macOS | `apple-music-discord-presence-macos-arm64.zip` | Apple Silicon, macOS 13+ |
| Windows | `apple-music-discord-presence-windows-x64.zip` | Windows 10 1809+ or Windows 11, x64 |

Extract the ZIP, keep the folder somewhere permanent, and run `Install.command` on macOS or `Install.cmd` on Windows.

## What changed

### Improved

- Replaced the compact feature diagram with a wide illustrated product journey and a four-column capability band.
- Rebuilt both platform cards around dedicated painterly scenes with stronger depth, contrast, and hover feedback.
- Added a light setup canvas and resilient per-step connectors that do not break between numbered nodes.
- Reframed the final CTA as a filled glass panel with an Apple Music × Discord lockup and balanced download actions.

## Verification

- macOS build, Swift helper, TypeScript, and automated tests.
- Manual Browser/IAB checks at 1440 × 1000 and 390 × 844.
- Verified feature, platform, setup, and CTA layouts; desktop and mobile step connectors; horizontal overflow; and browser console output.

## Known limitations

- The macOS build is not signed or notarized, so the first launch may require **Right-click → Open**.
- End-to-end playback still requires Apple Music and desktop Discord running on the user's computer.

## Upgrade notes

No migration required.
